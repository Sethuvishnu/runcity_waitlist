How delivery works.

Discover and clarify

Kickoff session, product walkthrough and review of existing materials. We pin down audience, main use case, differentiators, launch objective and calls to action.

Story and direction

A concise message map, narrative direction, visual references and an initial storyboard and page structure. Production starts after written approval.

Create and review

We produce the agreed video, web and campaign assets in milestones. You review at defined checkpoints with consolidated feedback from one decision-maker.

QA and handover

We check exports, responsive page behavior, links and agreed integrations, then hand over final files and a simple asset index.

Clear terms, no surprises.

Your responsibilities

Timely access to the product, accurate product information, brand assets, approved copy and legal claims, required logins, one feedback owner, and consolidated feedback within 3 business days of each review. Delayed inputs or approvals move the delivery schedule.

Not included unless separately quoted

Paid media, ad management, PR, influencer outreach, SEO campaigns, custom software or product engineering, CRM migrations, copy or legal compliance review, translation, extensive original photography, paid stock, music or voiceover licenses, hosting and domain fees, third-party subscriptions, unlimited revisions, ongoing maintenance.

The agreed-scope correction commitment

If a delivered item doesn't match the written brief or the approved storyboard or design direction, tell us in the relevant review round. We correct the mismatch at no additional creative fee until it meets the agreed acceptance criteria, as long as the request stays within approved scope.

This is a delivery-quality commitment, not a guarantee of sales, conversions, views, funding, launch performance or business results. New directions, new features, additional deliverables and post-approval changes are paid change requests.

Revisions and approvals

Each tier includes the revision rounds listed in its package. A round is one consolidated set of feedback from the client. Revisions refine the approved direction; they don't restart the project or replace the agreed concept. Additional rounds or scope changes need a written change order.

Scheduling

Projects are scheduled against real production capacity. A start date is reserved only after the agreement is signed and the booking payment is received. If you have a fixed launch date, contact us early so we can confirm feasibility. No artificial countdowns, and no claims of limited slots unless capacity genuinely requires it.
